# The Rim by Night: Awakened

Mage: The Awakening (second edition) in RimWorld. A colonist whose soul reaches one of the Supernal Realms Awakens. From then on she can cast spells with the ten Arcana, and every spell risks Paradox.

> **In beta.** Balance will change and bugs are likely. Keep a backup of any save you add the mod to.

## What's in it

- The Awakening, from omens, near death or a mentor, and the five Paths.
- Gnosis, Mana, Wisdom and the ten Arcana, with close to every spell in the core book.
- Rotes, Praxes and a spell builder for Potency, Duration, Scale, Range and Reach.
- Paradox, Conditions, Abyssal Tilts, Dissonance and Acts of Hubris.
- Hallows, tass, the Sanctum, Mage Sight and the Nimbus.
- Grimoires, Imbued Items and twenty Artifacts.
- The five Orders, Legacies, Merits, Obsessions and Aspirations.
- The Consilium: Status, services, the Lex Magica, hearings and the Duel Arcane.
- The Seers of the Throne, Banishers and the Abyss.
- Cabals, NPC mages who fight with spells, and colonies shared with vampires, werewolves and hunters.
